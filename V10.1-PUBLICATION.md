# V10.1 / Publication Engine 1.1

## Goal

Make the `.blog` part of Briefs.blog as serious as the intelligence engine.

A keyword is never enough to justify an article. The system requires a story opportunity backed by evidence, independent sourcing, novelty, audience relevance, originality and editorial quality.

## New public model

- **Living Briefs** — mutable current knowledge objects.
- **Briefings** — timestamped original articles and analysis.
- **Signals** — short change intelligence from the V10 Signals layer.

## Daily behavior

Every day the publication scheduler queues:
1. due watched keywords;
2. due published Briefings;
3. published Living Briefs whose verification window has expired.

The bounded publication worker processes that queue repeatedly via the protected publication cron. Unchanged content is marked revalidated without rewriting it. Changed content enters a verified update path.

## Human voice

Every article resolves a Reader Contract before composition and is scored against the Briefs voice contract. Stock AI phrases and repetitive prose patterns reduce the score or block publication.

## Originality

Briefs writes from structured claims rather than source prose. Source excerpts are held back from the optional writer adapter and used only for overlap checking afterward.

No system can truthfully promise mathematical proof of zero plagiarism. Briefs instead blocks suspicious overlap and requires review when exact or semantic-style phrase reuse is too high.

## Admin

`/admin/publication`

The desk contains:
- watched keywords;
- opportunities;
- research/story scores;
- article review queue;
- daily revalidation queue;
- update proposals.
