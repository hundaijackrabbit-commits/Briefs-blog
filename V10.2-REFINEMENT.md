# Briefs.blog V10.2 — Reader Intelligence & Editorial Refinement

V10.2 is a post-MVP refinement release. It does not reopen the ten-version roadmap.

## Why this release exists

V10.1 made Briefs capable of researching, drafting, publishing, checking originality, and revalidating articles. V10.2 improves the editorial decisions and the reader experience. The system now plans for a reader before it writes, evaluates whether the output actually fits that reader, and makes the blog choose among multiple defensible story angles rather than relying on one canned template.

## Brief answer refinement

The Brief path is now:

`query → intent → reader model → knowledge state → answer plan → grounded composition → answer-quality audit → response`

Reader models contain audience, goal, expertise, time budget, likely knowledge, needs, avoid-list, desired outcome and inference confidence. Answers can use an optional structured writer endpoint, but the endpoint receives structured claims and may only return claim IDs from that input. A deterministic reader-aware fallback is always available.

## Publication refinement

The article path is now:

`keyword → research → opportunity score → saturation check → multiple story angles → selected angle → story contract → draft → originality → audience fit → voice 2 → headline/specificity/evidence gates → review/publish`

The Story Contract records what the article is saying, why now, who it is for, what makes the Briefs version different, the strongest supporting claims, counter-claims, and what the article is not allowed to claim.

## Quality philosophy

Evidence quality and answer quality are intentionally separate. A perfectly sourced answer can still be a bad answer if it is indirect, generic, too technical, or written to nobody. Likewise, smooth prose cannot rescue weak evidence.

V10.2 records both dimensions and preserves the permanent safety rule: missing evidence narrows or blocks the output; it never licenses invention.

## Optional writer endpoints

- `BRIEFS_ANSWER_WRITER_URL`
- `BRIEFS_ANSWER_WRITER_TOKEN`
- `PUBLICATION_WRITER_URL`
- `PUBLICATION_WRITER_TOKEN`

These are provider-neutral interfaces. They are not required for operation.

## Database

Run `npm run refinement:db` after `DATABASE_URL` is configured. This adds story-angle candidates, story contracts, answer-quality evaluations, and expanded publication quality dimensions.
